Privacy-Preserving Infrastructure

Understanding the mathematical guarantees protecting FinTech records

Differential Privacy (DP-SGD)

In standard machine learning, model weights can memorize specific characteristics of raw datasets. An attacker who gets access to the model can perform Membership Inference Attacks to check if a specific client transaction was in the training set.

To prevent this, our clients use DP-SGD (Stochastic Gradient Descent with Differential Privacy). Every local gradient calculated from a transaction is clipped, limiting the maximum impact a single transaction can have on the model. We then inject calibrated Gaussian Noise:

$$W \leftarrow W - \eta \left( \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \text{clip}(g_i, C) + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 C^2) \right)$$

This guarantees mathematically that the outputs of the model are practically indistinguishable whether any single patient/transaction was included or not.

Federated Security Benefits

By sharing model updates instead of raw data, the FinTech Consortium gets the benefits of joint defense:

  • Zero Data Sharing: Your raw banking transfers never leave your bank's local firewall.
  • Collaborative Immunity: If one bank detects a zero-day attack, the updated global weights protect all other banks instantly without revealing details.
  • FedProx Optimization: Addresses Non-IID constraints, ensuring the local models don't collapse on their specific department configurations.
